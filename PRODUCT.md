# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: founders and business owners who run daily operations themselves and use AI chats that start as strangers each time. Secondary: operators running several ventures or client workspaces; technical users coordinating agents. Confirmed 2026-10-13: the website speaks to business owners first, developer proof sits lower.

## Product Purpose
Alacrán is a local-first desktop workspace (macOS, Linux) that gives AI a durable memory of a company. The owner describes the business once; it is written as plain files in a folder they own, handed to the connected AI agent before each task, and every proposed change returns as a diff for approval before it is saved. The website exists to explain this and earn a download.

## Positioning
The memory is plain files, git-versioned, executor-neutral (survives a model switch) and kept true by a refresh job, not a hosted vendor blob. A hosted competitor cannot truthfully copy "you own it, it outlives the model".

## Operating Context
Desktop app wrapping a local Next.js server on 127.0.0.1. Departments (Customers, Finances, Content & social, Messages), reusable workflows and daily scheduled jobs, an Approvals screen with diffs, Company Strings between workspaces, a voice assistant, bring-your-own agent (Claude Code, Google Antigravity, Kiro, Kimi, Codex). Connectors: Google (Gmail, Calendar), GitHub, Notion, Beeper chats, per-company MCP (Canva, Figma, Docusign, Vercel, freee).

## Capabilities and Constraints
Publication rules live in docs/website-copy.md and bind this redesign: do not imply real-time team collaboration or multi-user roles; "local-first" means record storage, connected AI and voice may process externally; app is free, providers may charge; sample data must be labelled "Example company · Sample data"; do not claim universal enforcement of all five approval categories; connector status labels must be honest.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Alacrán" in visible text, "Alacran" in filenames and download URLs. The mark is the red scorpion the desktop app uses (landing/logo.png, generated by scripts/generate-logo.py); it replaced the line-drawn mark on 2026-10-15 at the owner's request. Visual world (owner-pinned 2026-10-15): clean and light in the manner of laravel.com's home page and docs, with a Laravel-style hero animation; the owner found the carbon-copy receipt book on a green desk (2026-10-13) too busy to follow. The existing motion (reveals, tour auto-play, hero animation) is liked and stays, quieter. The Buy Me a Coffee widget (page id "alacran") is a standing part of every page.

## Evidence on Hand
Real app screenshots in landing/screens/*.webp (sample company, labelled). No customer testimonials, no benchmarks, no user counts: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with useful work and visible control; explain architecture second.
2. The owner's files are the product; show them as files.
3. Nothing is saved without a human seeing it first.
4. Be honest about what is local and what is external.
