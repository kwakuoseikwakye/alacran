Get started in minutes.

Alacrán runs locally on your Mac. Here’s what you need and how the first run goes. Full documentation is on the way.

Requirements

  • macOS 12 or later
  • Node.js installed
  • Claude Code CLI — bring your own Claude subscription
  • gog (Google CLI) — optional, to connect Gmail & Calendar

Alacrán checks for these on first launch and walks you through anything missing.

Your first run

1 · Install & open

Download, open the app; it starts locally and opens in your browser.

2 · Create a company

Scaffold your first company from the template in one click.

3 · Connect your agent

Point it at your Claude and link your tools.

4 · Run the work

Trigger a workflow — and approve the diff before anything is written.
