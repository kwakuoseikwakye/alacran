02 Install

Up and running in two minutes.

Pick your platform. Three steps, no account, nothing to configure.

  1. Download the app

    One universal build for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

    Download Alacrán.dmg

  2. Drag it to Applications

    Open the .dmg and drag Alacrán into your Applications folder, the same as any other Mac app.

  3. Run this once in Terminal

    xattr -cr "/Applications/Alacrán.app"

    The build is signed but not notarized, because notarizing needs a paid Apple Developer account. Without this, macOS quarantines the download and refuses to open it — often claiming the app is “damaged”. It isn’t; this just clears the quarantine flag macOS attaches to anything downloaded from the web.

    First install only · updates never need it

  1. Download the package

    A standard Debian package, built and published by CI for Debian and Ubuntu.

    Download Alacran.deb

  2. Install it

    sudo apt install ./Alacran.deb

    If your system doesn’t have apt, use sudo dpkg -i Alacran.deb && sudo apt -f install instead.

  3. Launch it

    Open Alacrán from your applications menu. Updates arrive in-app through a native password prompt — no terminal needed again.

  1. Clone and install

    git clone https://github.com/kwakuoseikwakye/alacran.git cd alacran npm install

  2. Run it

    npm run dev # http://localhost:3000

    Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript. npm test runs the full suite — no network, no real subprocesses.

  3. Or build your own package

    bash scripts/package-macos.sh # or package-linux.sh

    Produces the same .dmg or .deb the releases page ships.

Both builds bundle their own server: they start it on your machine and open your browser at 127.0.0.1. You’ll also need a coding-agent CLI — Claude Code is the default, and Codex, Aider and Antigravity all work. The complete guide walks through every screen assuming you’ve never touched an AI tool.

03 In plain English

Never used an AI tool before? Start right here.

You probably already know how to ask ChatGPT or Claude a question. The catch is that it starts every conversation as a total stranger. It doesn’t know what you sell, who your customers are, or how you like things written. As a result, you end up typing the same background paragraph over and over before it can actually be useful.

Alacrán is a small app for your computer that fixes exactly that. You write down the basics of your business once in ordinary words. From then on, your AI reads that first, every single time, and gets straight to the actual work.

Without it

  • You re-explain the business in every new chat
  • You copy answers out of a chat window by hand
  • Last month’s good draft is lost in old conversations
  • The tone comes out slightly wrong every time

With it

  • It already knows the business before you ask
  • The work is saved where you can easily find it later
  • Anything it did well becomes a one-click repeat
  • You read the change and approve it, or you don’t

08 Privacy & Security

In plain English:
what leaves my computer?

Most apps say “we take privacy seriously”. Here is the actual architecture instead. Alacrán runs as a small server on your own computer, reachable only from your machine. We do not operate a server your business data could reach, so there is no database of ours with your work in it, and no admin panel anyone here could open.

There is no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting anywhere in the app. Not disabled by default. Not present at all.

09 Questions

The things people actually ask.

Straight answers, including the awkward ones.

I’ve never used an AI tool. Is this going to be over my head?

No. If you can fill in a form and read a paragraph before clicking Yes, you can use Alacrán. There is no code, no prompt writing, and no jargon to learn.

Setup is four plain questions about your business, in ordinary words. After that, running a job is pressing a button and reading what comes back. Our complete guide walks through every screen assuming you’ve never touched an AI tool in your life.

Can you see my business data?

No, and not because we promise not to. We have no way to.

Alacrán runs on your computer, listening only to your own machine. We don’t operate a server your company data could be sent to, which means there is no database on our side holding your work and no internal tool any employee of ours could use to look at it. The data sits in a folder you chose, on your laptop.

Do I have to pay for AI on top of this?

You need an account with whichever AI a workspace uses. By default that’s your own Claude subscription from Anthropic, which many people already have, and it’s the option we’ve run the longest.

We deliberately don’t resell AI usage, so there is no bill from us at all — the app itself is free and open source. You pay your AI provider for the AI, and nothing to us, ever.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Then you don’t approve it, and nothing happens. This is the core design of the app rather than a safety feature bolted on.

When a job finishes, nothing has been saved yet. You see exactly what it wants to change, laid out clearly, and you either accept it or throw it away. If you accept something and regret it later, every approval is saved as a checkpoint you can roll back to.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Alacrán is free and open source under the MIT licence. There is no account, no licence key, no trial clock and no paid tier. Simply download it and it works.

You do still pay whoever provides your AI (an Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google subscription, or nothing at all if you point it at a local model). That bill is between you and them; we never see it.
