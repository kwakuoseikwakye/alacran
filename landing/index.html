Just shipped

We ship fast, and we fix what we find.

Alacrán is being built in the open, one small release at a time. Here’s what changed most recently.

New

Never lose a company again

Back up any company to a private GitHub repo in one click, then restore it onto a new Mac just as easily. Switching laptops used to mean starting over. Now it doesn’t.

Fixed

Your data now survives app updates

We found and closed a bug where updating Alacrán could wipe a company’s data. Everything now lives safely outside the app itself, and existing companies were migrated automatically, with nothing lost.

New

Skills, made obvious

The Skills page now shows exactly which of your companies already has a skill installed, so you’re never left guessing what’s already running where.

Improved

A setup wizard that explains itself

Every question in company setup now comes with a plain-language example, so you’re never stuck staring at a field wondering what it actually wants from you.

Local by design

It runs where you do.

Alacrán lives on your Mac. Your company’s data, your agent, and your keys all stay right there on your machine. No cloud tenant, no black box.
