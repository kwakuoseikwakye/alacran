    Your download has started. One more step on a Mac.

    Apple has not notarized Alacrán, because that needs a paid Apple Developer account. Until you do this once, macOS may block the app, often with the message "Alacrán is damaged and can't be opened". It is not damaged.

    1. Install. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Alacrán into Applications.
    2. Run this in Terminal, once. It clears the quarantine flag macOS puts on downloaded files.
      xattr -cr "/Applications/Alacran.app"
    3. Open Alacrán from Applications.

    You only need this for the first install. Updates inside the app do not need it. Prefer to read more? Read the setup guide.
