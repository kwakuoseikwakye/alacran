Before you start

A supported computer.

A Mac running macOS 12 or later, or a Debian or Ubuntu Linux computer. Choose the download that matches your Mac’s processor.

An AI account for AI tasks.

The current guided setup starts with Claude Code. You will need a compatible account and provider access. Provider subscriptions, usage limits, and charges apply separately.

A short description of your business.

A sentence or two is enough to begin. You can improve the details as you work.

1. Install and open Alacrán.

On Mac, open the downloaded .dmg and drag Alacrán into Applications. On Linux, install the downloaded .deb package using your package installer.

If installation is blocked, read the release-specific installation guidance before continuing. Some current Mac builds require an additional setup step because they are not notarized by Apple.

You do not need an Alacrán account to create your local workspace.

2. Choose your starting goal.

Choose the work you want help with: running your business, building software, winning customers, or growing your audience. Read the trust information so you understand local storage, provider access, and review expectations.

Your starting goal helps shape the workspace. It does not lock you into one way of working.

3. Connect your AI.

Follow the AI setup screen to install and sign in to the supported agent. Return to Alacrán and use Re-check to confirm the connection.

You can choose Explore without AI if you want to look around and add records first. Connect an agent when you are ready for AI-generated work.

4. Create your company.

Give the workspace a name and choose an appropriate starter pack. Alacrán creates a company folder for its facts, notes, decisions, and operational records.

If you already have a suitable project folder or backup, use the available import or restore option. Existing folders may follow a different onboarding path.

5. Describe how your business works.

Answer the guided questions:

  • What does your business do, and who does it serve?
  • Who is involved: customers, team members, partners, or suppliers?
  • How does work move from a request to a finished result?
  • What takes up the most time today?

Keep unknown details visibly incomplete rather than filling them with assumptions.

On Check & save, choose Let Alacrán write it up to prepare a draft for approval, or Save my answers as they are to keep your own wording.

6. Review your first draft.

Open Approvals and inspect the proposed company information. Choose Review & approve, Ask for changes, or Discard.

Your company profile influences later tasks, so check its facts carefully. Some previews currently show structured text such as name: value rather than a formatted summary.

7. Ask a useful first question.

Review the result before using it outside the workspace.

If something needs attention

The agent is installed but not connected.

Complete its sign-in, check provider access, and run Re-check again.

A job finishes without a result.

Check the run status and provider usage limits. Review the reported error before retrying.

A connection is missing.

Open Connections or System Doctor to inspect requirements and suggested fixes.

The app looks too technical.

Keep Advanced mode off while you learn the main workflows. Some current setup and review screens may still expose technical details.

I closed the window, but work is continuing.

Closing the window can leave Alacrán running in the background. Use Quit when you want to stop the application.

ALACRÁN

Your download has started. One more step on a Mac.

Apple has not notarized Alacrán, because that needs a paid Apple Developer account. Until you do this once, macOS may block the app, often with the message "Alacrán is damaged and can't be opened". It is not damaged.

  1. Install. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Alacrán into Applications.
  2. Run this in Terminal, once. It clears the quarantine flag macOS puts on downloaded files.
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Alacran.app"
  3. Open Alacrán from Applications.

You only need this for the first install. Updates inside the app do not need it. Prefer to read more? Read the setup guide.
