# Surface brief: public website

Scope: every public page of alacran.app (home, get started, how to use, docs, starter packs, integrations, use cases, four department pages, privacy, terms). Home is Persuade; every other page is Read.

Audience, job, action: business owners deciding whether to download; then readers following setup and docs. Action: download Alacrán, or read the setup guide. Proof on hand: real app screenshots in landing/screens (sample company, labelled). No testimonials, counts or benchmarks exist; none may be invented. Copy is owned by docs/website-copy.md and is not reworded by this redesign.

Chosen direction: pinned by the owner on 2026-10-15: laravel.com's clean look and hero animation, with the existing motion kept but quieter. Replaces the receipt-book world (owner: "a lot going on, hard to find or follow").

Memorable moment: the hero illustration. Isometric slabs (one per department) with a red memory slab on top; a line draws in, an agent chip travels along it, a diff appears, an APPROVED stamp lands.

Unresolved: none. Open items to hand over: tour uses interim screenshots; og.png still shows the old look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One calm page that says what Alacrán is in a single read: a large light headline, one illustration that shows the mechanism (company files, agent task, diff, human approval), one primary action. It refuses the crowded desk: no scattered floating chips, tickers, number strips, kickers, or stamps on every section.

OWN-WORLD: Warm grey ground (#f4f2ee), white sheets, hairline warm rules (#e3dfd7), near-black ink, one accent, the scorpion red taken from the desktop logo (#d6202f for fills and buttons, #e22a3f on the hero slab, deep #a81424 for text on grey). Onest at weight 300 for display with tight tracking, JetBrains Mono only for file paths and commands. Isometric slabs in white and light grey with one red slab. 10px radii, 1px borders, low soft shadows. The red scorpion raster from the desktop app sits at the left of the nav and footer.

STORY: The visitor understands that Alacrán keeps the company as plain files they own, lets an AI work on them, and saves nothing until they approve it. They believe it because the hero shows the mechanism and the tour shows the real app. They download, or open the setup guide.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (logo, four links, Guides menu, red Download button). Below, at 1440 wide: left column holds the light headline (about 62px at 1440, one block per sentence), a 20px lede, two buttons (red Download, white See it in action) and one quiet free-for-Mac-and-Linux line. Right column is the isometric stack, about 600px, its line animating. Nothing else is on screen.

FORM: User-pinned reference, laravel.com home and docs, not drawn from the seed list; no seed key (no roll was run because the brief pinned the direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
